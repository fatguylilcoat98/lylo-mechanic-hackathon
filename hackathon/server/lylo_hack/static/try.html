LYLO Mechanic

Powered by Meta AI. No adapter needed. Describe what your car is doing, add a trouble code or a dash photo.

Web demo of the LYLO Android app. Without live sensor data this is a lower-confidence check and never says "safe to drive". Causes are the AI's guesses, labeled as guesses. The safety level comes from fixed rules and the AI can only raise it. Photos are sent to Meta AI to be read and are not stored. Limited uses per day.
